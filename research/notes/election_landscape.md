## Exact dates, eligibility and ID rules (Elections BC)

### Takeaway
Premier David Eby called the election on 2026-09-22 for Saturday 2026-10-24. Advance voting is Oct 16-21, vote-by-mail requests close Oct 18, and mail packages must reach Elections BC by 8 p.m. PT on Oct 24. The municipal general local elections are Oct 17, one week earlier, and overlap the advance voting period.

### Cited Findings
- Election Day is October 24, 2026; general local (municipal) elections are Oct 17, 2026 - [Elections BC home](https://elections.bc.ca/)
- Advance voting Oct 16-21, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. local time (not every location open every day); Final Voting Day Oct 24, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Pacific; district electoral offices accept voters until 4 p.m. on Oct 24 - [Elections BC, Ways to Vote](https://elections.bc.ca/voting/ways-to-vote/)
- Vote by mail: last day to request a package online or by phone is Oct 18; completed package must be received by Elections BC by 8 p.m. PT Oct 24. Unregistered or out-of-date voters must supply ID by secure upload or photocopies with the package; voters write birthdate on the certification envelope and sign a declaration - [Elections BC, Ways to Vote](https://elections.bc.ca/voting/ways-to-vote/)
- Eligibility: Canadian citizen, 18 or older on Oct 24, 2026, and B.C. resident since April 23, 2026 - [Elections BC, Who Can Vote](https://elections.bc.ca/voting/who-can-vote/)
- ID: voters must prove identity and residential address. Option 1: one piece of government photo ID showing name and address (BC driver's licence, BCID, BC Services Card with photo, other B.C./Canada government card with name, photo and address, Certificate of Indian Status). Option 2: two pieces, both with name, at least one with current address; electronic documents accepted; handwritten info generally not accepted. Option 3: vouching by a registered voter in the district or a relative (spouse, parent, grandparent, adult child, adult grandchild, adult sibling) or a personal-care decision maker; a non-relative may vouch for only one voter - [Elections BC, Voter ID](https://elections.bc.ca/2026-provincial-election/voter-id/)
- Snap election called Sept 22, 2026 by Premier Eby, framed around US (Trump) trade tensions; the NDP held a one-seat majority; the Lieutenant Governor agreed to dissolve - [CTV News, Sept 22](https://www.ctvnews.ca/vancouver/article/snap-election-call-coming-bc-premier-addressing-province-tuesday/) (headline only retrieved; quote "existential threat"), [Bloomberg, Sept 22](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/british-columbia-leader-eby-calls-snap-election-to-cement-power-amid-us-tariffs), [National Observer, Sept 22](https://www.nationalobserver.com/2026/09/22/news/bcs-eby-calls-snap-election-conservative-party-flails)

- Candidate nominations closed 1 p.m. PT Saturday Oct 3 - [Western Standard Day 8](https://www.westernstandard.news/bc/day-8-of-the-2026-bc-election-carney-comes-to-town/77389)
- Final count Nov 6-10 and return of writs Nov 18 (secondary) - [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_British_Columbia_general_election)
- The call cancelled the Abbotsford-Mission by-election (scheduled Sept 26) and an unscheduled Vancouver-Strathcona by-election - [National Observer via search summary](https://www.nationalobserver.com/2026/09/22/news/bcs-eby-calls-snap-election-conservative-party-flails); [Wikipedia 43rd Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/43rd_Parliament_of_British_Columbia)

- Mainstreet (Sept 30-Oct 1): snap-election approval 29.4%, disapproval 59.3% - [Western Standard Day 15](https://www.westernstandard.news/bc/day-15-of-the-2026-bc-provincial-election-a-lead-a-stage-a-hospital-and-a-curling-rink/77617)

## Key events 2024-2026

### Takeaway
Major events: the NDP-Green accord (Dec 2024, ended Feb 2026), consumer carbon tax repeal (Apr 1, 2025), Bills 14 and 15 passed May 2025 on a Speaker tie-break, the Cowichan title ruling (Aug 2025) and DRIPA amendment fallout, and the end of the decriminalization pilot (Jan 31, 2026).

### Cited Findings
- NDP-Green accord signed Dec 12, 2024; Greens declined to renew on Feb 9, 2026 - [Wikipedia 43rd Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/43rd_Parliament_of_British_Columbia) (secondary, cites CBC)
- Consumer carbon tax: Eby announced the repeal at a Surrey town hall on Mar 14, 2025; legislation passed overnight Mar 31; tax ended Apr 1, 2025; industrial carbon pricing continues - [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/vancouver/article/bc-ends-its-carbon-tax-on-consumers-after-marathon-debate-in-legislature/); [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/11097966/bc-consumer-carbon-tax-april-1); [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.7493181)
