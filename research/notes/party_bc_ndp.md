# BC NDP: 2026 platform and record in government 2017-2026

Research date: 2026-10-09. Election: Oct 24, 2026 (snap, called Sept 22). Source-quality note: bcndp.ca pages are party press releases (partisan claims, labelled as such). Several major outlets (CBC, Times Colonist) returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch, so some CBC items are known only from search-result summaries and are flagged. bcballot.ca and voche.me are third-party aggregators, not primary.

## 1. Election context and 2026 platform status (what has been released)

### Takeaway
As of Oct 9, 2026 the BC NDP has not published a single consolidated platform document; commitments come out as daily campaign press releases. The one aggregator that tracked them found no NDP commitments yet on education/child care, transportation, Indigenous relations or government/budgets. Treat the list in section 2 as partial.

## 2. 2026 platform commitments by issue (stated promises, as of Oct 9)

### Takeaway
The NDP's announced 2026 package centres on: a tax on incomes above $190,405 (about $1B/year by 2028/29), a 10 cent/L fuel tax cut plus a regulator "price guard", higher speculation tax and a new Unsold Condo Tax, diagnostic/wait-time and seniors' care expansions, an ICBC rate freeze to 2030, trade-war small-business aid and "Built in B.C. and Canada" procurement, and critical-minerals/transmission fast-tracking.

### Cited Findings
Taxes and fiscal
- Raise the top two income tax brackets by 2 points and add a new bracket above $1M, from the 2027 tax year: $190,405-$265,545 from 16.8% to 18.8%; $265,545-$1M from 20.5% to 22.5%; over $1M new 24.5%. Party says 3.88% of earners affected. Projected new revenue $225M (2026/27), $925M (2027/28), $1,000M (2028/29). - [BC NDP release Oct 4](https://www.bcndp.ca/releases/build-bc-strong-eby-will-ask-wealthy-pitch-bit-more); [Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/david-eby-bc-ndp-higher-income-taxes-top-earners-platform)
- Daily Hive additionally reports a freeze on inflation indexing of tax brackets for 2027-2030 with indexing resuming in 2031; the bcndp.ca release page, as read, did not mention it. Conflict/unverified. - [Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/david-eby-bc-ndp-higher-income-taxes-top-earners-platform)
- Revenue wording: the release says "a billion dollars in new revenue each year" but its own table reaches $1B only in 2028/29. - [BC NDP release Oct 4](https://www.bcndp.ca/releases/build-bc-strong-eby-will-ask-wealthy-pitch-bit-more)
- Conservative response: leader Doerkson pledges no new provincial taxes; says the tax would drive doctors out. - [Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/david-eby-bc-ndp-higher-income-taxes-top-earners-platform); [BOE Report](https://boereport.com/2026/10/01/b-c-s-eby-promises-relief-at-gas-pumps-while-conservative-doerkson-praises-pipeline/amp/)
- Fiscal update (Sept 2026): deficit $13.8B this year, $12.7B in 2027/28, $12.0B in 2028/29; Daily Hive cites total debt reaching $180.9B this fiscal year. No NDP balanced-budget date found. - [Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/david-eby-bc-ndp-higher-income-taxes-top-earners-platform); [CFJC](https://cfjctoday.com/?p=1125078)

Affordability
- Suspend 10 cents/L of the provincial motor fuel tax (gasoline and diesel); create a BC Utilities Commission "price guard" to set rules and cap excess margins (reported as a maximum price updated weekly). Party claims combined savings "up to 30 cents/L". Cost reported by Eby as about $250M (from BOE Report); start date and duration not stated in release. - [BC NDP release Oct 1](https://www.bcndp.ca/releases/ebys-plan-will-cut-gas-prices-30-cents-litre-crack-down-gouging); [BOE Report](https://boereport.com/2026/10/01/b-c-s-eby-promises-relief-at-gas-pumps-while-conservative-doerkson-praises-pipeline/amp/)
- Grocery: cap retail margins on milk, eggs, cheese, butter, poultry for corporate retailers; per-unit shelf pricing; ban anti-competitive real-estate controls; ban AI/"surveillance" algorithmic pricing; partner with Ottawa on a Lower Mainland wholesale food terminal. - [BC Ballot summary of Oct 2 release](https://bcballot.ca/platforms/bc-ndp/) (aggregator; release URL: https://www.bcndp.ca/releases/eby-will-take-grocery-giants-protect-families-rising-costs, not read directly)
- Extend the ICBC rate freeze three more years to spring 2030 (party says rates frozen seven years; claims average driver saves about $500/year from Enhanced Care and cleanup). No cost given. Party alleges the Conservatives would open auto insurance to private competition, citing a letter from Doerkson to the BC Trial Lawyers Association; Conservatives call the NDP claims "inventing numbers" (Western Standard). - [BC NDP release Oct 8](https://www.bcndp.ca/releases/eby-freeze-car-insurance-rates-another-three-years); [Western Standard](https://www.westernstandard.news/amp/story/bc/day-16-of-the-2026-bc-provincial-election-eby-shifts-to-running-against-his-own-governments-record/77655) (partisan outlet)
- No new Metro Vancouver TransLink vehicle levy: Eby on Oct 2, "We are not going ahead with the vehicle levy." The province has committed to legislation in 2027 for a new TransLink revenue source starting 2028 (not specified); TransLink needs at least $112M/year once provincial subsidies end; province gave $312M operating subsidies in 2025 through end of 2027. - [Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/bc-ndp-david-eby-translink-vehicle-levy)
- The 2024 $1,000 grocery rebate promise was paused in Feb 2025 citing tariff uncertainty (about $1.8B cost); some outlets describe it as cancelled. Interim Conservative leader cited it as a credibility issue in 2026. - [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/11017886/bc-pauses-tax-rebate-donald-trump-tariffs/amp); [Money.ca](https://money.ca/news/bc-scraps-grocery-rebate); [BOE Report](https://boereport.com/2026/10/01/b-c-s-eby-promises-relief-at-gas-pumps-while-conservative-doerkson-praises-pipeline/amp/)

Housing
- Raise speculation and vacancy tax by one point: 1% to 2% (domestic), 4% to 5% (foreign); extra revenue to affordable homes. New Unsold Condo Tax on finished condos unsold more than a year: no tax in year 1, 2% in year 2, 3% in year 3, rising 1 point per year; party says it could free up to 5,000 condos. No revenue estimate. Health

Release states surgery waits range from 10 to 50+ weeks depending on surgeon. - [BC NDP release Oct 6](https://www.bcndp.ca/releases/getting-care-sooner-eby-will-cut-wait-times-add-new-diagnostic-equipment)

- Triple US health-worker recruitment, expand into Republican-led states; reported cost $15M (voche.me, unverified); loan forgiveness and moving support for Canadian health workers returning from the US. - [BC Ballot summary](https://bcballot.ca/platforms/bc-ndp/); [voche.me](https://voche.me/bc/provincial-2026/parties/bc-ndp/platform) (aggregators)

Economy, trade, resources

- Pipeline: no commitment found. Drugs and public safety

- Public safety (Oct 5): $500,000/year for three years to the Western Jewish Community Security Network; B.C. police summit on extremism if re-elected. - [BC NDP release](https://www.bcndp.ca/releases/bc-ndp-commits-jewish-community-security-investment-and-summit-confront-organized-hate) (via BC Ballot; not read directly)

Other

### Inferences

- Price controls (fuel margins, grocery margins) point toward more market intervention than in prior terms.

## 3. Record: housing and affordability (2017-2026)

### Takeaway
The NDP created the speculation and vacancy tax (2018), a flipping tax (2025), and mandated density via Bills 44 and 47 (Nov 2023) and BC Builds. Starts and rents have moved but the evidence is mixed: rents are falling and vacancy is at a 37-year high in Metro Vancouver, but starts are slipping and CMHC forecasts a decline through 2028.

### Cited Findings
- Speculation and vacancy tax introduced 2018; government says over $300M collected and at least 20,000 homes freed up in Metro Vancouver (government claim). - [CTV News](https://bc.ctvnews.ca/b-c-premier-to-make-announcement-about-housing-speculation-1.6783865)
- Homes for People plan (April 2023): $4B over three years, $12B over a decade; legislation for up to four units on traditional single lots; flipping tax announced; BC Builds launched. Flipping tax on homes sold within two years, effective Jan 1, 2025 (as planned in Budget 2024). - [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.6799881); [BC Gov](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024FIN0009-000247)
- Bill 44 (small-scale multi-unit housing) and Bill 47 (transit-oriented areas within 800 m of rapid transit and 400 m of major bus exchanges, removing most parking minimums) received royal assent Nov 30, 2023; some municipalities pushed back. - [MLT Aikins](https://www.mltaikins.com/insights/update-bills-44-and-47-receive-royal-assent-in-british-columbia)
- Outcomes: Metro Vancouver starts 27,185 in 2025 vs 28,112 in 2024; B.C. CMHC urban starts 41,532 (down about 5%); BC Housing registry 47,858 units (up 4.7%, driven by purpose-built rentals); Metro Vancouver purpose-built vacancy 3.7% in 2025 (highest since 1988, from 1.6%); 2-bedroom turnover rent $2,883 to $2,696. CMHC July 22, 2026 outlook: Vancouver starts 26,000-27,000 in 2026 falling to 20,500-22,000 by 2028, condo construction historically low. - [Northern Beat summary of CMHC data](https://northernbeat.ca/election-news/british-columbias-housing-market-march-2026-overview/); [Business Examiner](https://businessexaminer.ca/be-magazine/item/cmhc-summer-2026-outlook-vancouver-housing-starts-condo/) (secondary reporting of CMHC)
- ICBC: Enhanced Care (no-fault) reform; rates frozen; party says average driver saves about $500/year (party claim). Horgan government eliminated MSP premiums effective Jan 1, 2020 (net $800M tax cut, up to $900/year for individuals), replaced by an employer payroll levy. - [BC NDP](https://www.bcndp.ca/releases/eby-freeze-car-insurance-rates-another-three-years); [CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/b-c-says-bye-bye-to-medical-services-plan-premiums-1.5386038)
- Minimum wage $18.25/hour as of June 1, 2026 (from $17.85, indexed to 2025 inflation). - [BC Gov](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026LBR0021-000581)
- Child care: government reports 15,300 $10-a-day spaces (spring 2025) and a goal of 20,000 by March 31, 2026; average daily cost for under-5s fell to $19 from $54 (ministry figures). - [BC Gov](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025ECC0015-000242); [Deputy Minister's Bulletin](https://blog.gov.bc.ca/ecc-dm-bulletin/article/new-10-a-day-childcarebc-centres-opening/)

### Inferences
- Record suggests a continued mix of demand-side taxes plus mandated density; 2026 proposals to raise the speculation tax and add a condo tax fit that pattern.
- Starts forecasts mean supply gains are not assured regardless of party.

### Gaps
- Whether the 20,000 $10-a-day spaces target was met by March 31, 2026: not confirmed.
- Eby's housing minister dates: sources not found (he was housing minister before becoming premier in Nov 2022; exact dates not verified here). Primary CMHC pages not read directly.
- 2025 average home price and rent index data not obtained.

## 4. Record: health care

### Takeaway
Spending and some capacity metrics are up, but about 23% of British Columbians lack a primary care provider (up 45% since 2017). The family doctor payment model has recruited and retained doctors per the government, while critics say it raised incomes without improving access.

### Cited Findings
- New longitudinal family physician payment model launched Feb 1, 2023; designed for a full-time family doctor with 1,250 patients and average gross pay about $385,000 vs $250,000 previously. - [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/9527599/bc-new-doctor-pay-model-uptake); [Tyee](https://thetyee.ca/News/2026/05/31/BC-Family-Doctor-Crisis-Gets-Even-Worse/)
- As of end April 2026, 1,259,425 people (about 23% of 5.7M) unattached, vs 897,000 (18.2%) in 2017; 345,000 on the Health Connect Registry (March 2026); median wait 295 days; over 4,000 attachments weekly. CCPA's Andrew Longhurst argues the model raised incomes without improving access; Health Minister Josie Osborne disputes and cites "real progress". - [Tyee, May 31, 2026](https://thetyee.ca/News/2026/05/31/BC-Family-Doctor-Crisis-Gets-Even-Worse/)
- Party claims: record 382,000 surgeries last year; MRI exams up 108% and CT up 61% since 2017 (no baseline given); surgical waits 10 to 50+ weeks. Eby called 50-week waits "totally unacceptable." - [BC NDP release Oct 6](https://www.bcndp.ca/releases/getting-care-sooner-eby-will-cut-wait-times-add-new-diagnostic-equipment); [CFJC](https://cfjctoday.com/?p=1125080)
- Health Ministry budget about $36B (Feb 2026 budget). - [Tyee](https://thetyee.ca/News/2026/05/31/BC-Family-Doctor-Crisis-Gets-Even-Worse/)

### Inferences
- The 2026 plan (diagnostics, waitlist coordination, US recruitment, seniors' care) extends existing approaches; the funding source is the new top-bracket tax.

### Gaps
- Independent 2025-26 surgical wait time data, ER closure counts, and nurse-to-patient ratio implementation status not found.

## 5. Record: drugs and addiction

### Takeaway
The NDP moved from decriminalization (2023) to restricting public use (2023-24) to ending the pilot (Jan 31, 2026), and from take-home safer supply to witnessed dosing (2025), while expanding involuntary care. Drug deaths fell in 2025 but remain above 1,800.

### Cited Findings
- Decriminalization began Jan 31, 2023 (up to 2.5 g cumulative of opioids, crack and powder cocaine, methamphetamine, MDMA). Nov 2023: public-use ban legislation, leaving private homes, shelters, health sites. April 26, 2024: Eby asked Ottawa to ban public consumption. Jan 14, 2026: Health Minister Osborne said B.C. would not seek an extension; pilot ended Jan 31, 2026. Eby: "We are not going back to the old policy of decriminalized, public drug use." - [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/11601344/bc-decriminalized-public-drug-use-premier); [BCCSU](https://www.bccsu.ca/blog/news/end-of-b-c-drug-decriminalization-pilot-marks-a-setback-for-drug-policy-reform-in-canada-say-advocates/); [Castanet](https://www.castanetkamloops.net/news/BC/484916/Drug-decriminalization-pilot-will-continue-says-Eby)
- Safer supply: Feb 2025 witnessed dosing required for new clients; from Dec 30, 2025 all prescribed-alternative patients need witnessed dosing. Eby: "an unfortunate thing but a necessary thing". Dr. Mark Tyndall said it effectively ends the program; Conservatives called for an inquiry. - [BCCSU](https://www.bccsu.ca/blog/news/b-c-to-require-most-drug-users-receiving-safe-supply-to-have-their-doses-witnessed/); [CTV](https://guelph.ctvnews.ca/vancouver/article/relief-and-frustration-from-critics-as-bc-amends-safer-supply-policy)
- Involuntary care: announced Sept 2024; 2026 release says two facilities add 132 mental-health treatment beds including 60 in Surrey (up to $92M budget) and 72 in Prince George. B.C. Supreme Court (Justice Blake, reported July 31, 2026) found Mental Health Act provisions allowing involuntary treatment unconstitutional; province has six months to amend; AG reviewing. - [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.7324079); [BC Gov release 2026HLTH0029](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HLTH0029-000808) (via search summary; fetch failed); [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/12005744/sections-bc-involuntary-care-rules-found-unconstitutional/)
- Unregulated drug deaths: 2022 2,383; 2023 2,511 (record); 2024 2,315 (as reported with 2025 comparison); 2025 1,826 preliminary (down about 21%, first sub-2,000 year since 2020); Fraser Health 533 and Vancouver Coastal 484 in 2025; Jan 2026 150 deaths. - [CTV](https://bc.ctvnews.ca/2-511-people-died-from-toxic-drugs-in-b-c-last-year-coroner-says-1.6740134); [Hashilthsa/Coroners summary](https://hashilthsa.com/node/20065) (secondary; 2024 total of 2,315 only appeared in this comparison)

### Inferences
- Direction since 2024 is toward enforcement, witnessed treatment and compulsory care; further movement that way is more likely than a return to decriminalization. The Surrey 60-bed unit is local relevance.

### Gaps
- Coroners Service primary dashboard not read; the 2024 figure is derived from the 21% comparison. NDP 2026 platform on a replacement for decriminalization: none found beyond Kelowna involuntary care.

## 6. Record: DRIPA and reconciliation

### Takeaway
BC passed DRIPA unanimously in 2019 (Horgan, NDP). After the Dec 2025 Court of Appeal ruling that its obligations are enforceable, Eby moved to amend or suspend it, then backed off in April 2026 amid First Nations opposition and a lack of caucus votes. A fix is promised but unspecified; the Conservatives pledge repeal.

### Cited Findings
- DRIPA passed unanimously 2019; Haida Title Lands "Rising Tide" agreement legislation introduced April 2024 recognizing Haida Aboriginal title over Haida Gwaii. - [Narwhal](https://thenarwhal.ca/bc-declaration-act-explainer/); [CTV](https://bc.ctvnews.ca/historic-law-recognizing-haida-aboriginal-title-introduced-in-b-c-legislature-1.6857663)
- Aug 2025: B.C. Supreme Court affirmed Cowichan Tribes Aboriginal title over about 300 ha in Richmond; province and Canada appealing. Dec 2025: Court of Appeal (Gitxaala) held the mineral claims regime inconsistent with UNDRIP and DRIPA obligations enforceable; Eby called the exposure "unlimited legal liability". - [Narwhal](https://thenarwhal.ca/bc-declaration-act-explainer/); [CFJC](https://cfjctoday.com/2026/02/06/b-c-asks-top-court-to-hear-dripa-appeal-saying-core-democratic-values-at-stake/)
- Jan 2026: Eby announced DRIPA would be amended; April 1 called change "non-negotiable"; proposed suspending parts for up to three years; First Nations Leadership Council said it would legally challenge; Grand Chief Stewart Phillip said leaders lost confidence. April 13: House Leader Farnworth said no bill that week and it would not be a confidence matter (Indigenous MLA Joan Phillip would not vote for it; one-seat majority). April 20: Eby said no changes this spring, joint statement with FNLC to seek a solution over the summer; amendments not before October. - [Tyee](https://thetyee.ca/News/2026/04/14/BC-Backs-Off-DRIPA-Amendments/); [Narwhal](https://thenarwhal.ca/bc-declaration-act-explainer/); [Turtle Island News](https://theturtleislandnews.com/index.php/2026/04/02/premier-eby-says-changing-dripa-is-non-negotiable-and-will-be-pushed-into-law/)
