## Health care: family doctors, ER closures, wait times, nurse ratios, Surrey hospitals, private delivery, each party's plan

### Takeaway
Primary-care attachment is the sharpest factual dispute: the NDP reports about 1 million more people attached since 2018, while the Health Minister's own end-of-April 2026 figure is 1,259,425 British Columbians (about 23%) without a regular provider. The NDP's 2026 health platform is clinics, diagnostics, home care and a new top-earner tax; the Conservatives promise ER-keeping measures, a $50 million rural fund, a 100-day wait-time commission and "universal and publicly funded" care (no private-clinic plank found in 2026, unlike the 2024 Rustad plan); the Greens promise Community Health Centres in every riding and public-only delivery.

### Cited Findings

Family doctor attachment (RECORD / data)
- 1,259,425 people were not attached to a primary care provider at end of April 2026 per Health Minister Josie Osborne, about 23% of BC's 5.7 million; the Canadian Community Health Survey counted 897,000 (18.2%) when the NDP took office in 2017. The article describes this as a 45% rise but the arithmetic is about 40%; the discrepancy is unexplained (different methods likely). - [The Tyee, 2026-05-31](https://thetyee.ca/News/2026/05/31/BC-Family-Doctor-Crisis-Gets-Even-Worse/)
- Health Connect Registry: over 4,000 attachments per week; 345,000 still waiting on the registry as of March 2026; median wait to attach is 295 days provincewide (Northern 198, Vancouver Coastal 171, Fraser 241, Island 388, Interior 477 days); the minister declined to give maximum waits. Average panel is about 1,250 patients per physician. - [The Tyee, 2026-05-31](https://thetyee.ca/News/2026/05/31/BC-Family-Doctor-Crisis-Gets-Even-Worse/)
- Government release 2026-04-01 says more than 600,000 people have been connected to a family doctor or nurse practitioner since 2023 and about 77% of residents have a provider (via search summary) - [news.gov.bc.ca 2026HLTH0018](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HLTH0018-000361) (my direct fetch failed on a certificate error).
- NDP campaign page claims about 1 million more people attached to a family doctor or nurse practitioner since 2018 (700,000 in the last three years), over 1,400 family doctors hired (+33% since 2017), primary care nurse practitioners more than tripled. Party claim, not independently verified. - [BC NDP, Action for You](https://www.bcndp.ca/action-for-you)
- Conflict: NDP "attached" claims versus the Minister's unattached figure are not necessarily contradictory (population growth, attrition, different denominators), but the Tyee reports the unattached count rose, not fell, since 2017. - [The Tyee](https://thetyee.ca/News/2026/05/31/BC-Family-Doctor-Crisis-Gets-Even-Worse/)

Longitudinal Family Physician (LFP) payment model (RECORD)
- Launched February 2023, moving family doctors from fee-for-service to a time-, complexity- and panel-based model; 2,024 doctors enrolled in the first month; over 4,300 had enrolled by the second anniversary (Feb/Mar 2025). - [Doctors of BC](https://www.doctorsofbc.ca/compensation/2025/lfp-payment-model-celebrates-its-second-anniversary)
- At launch the model was expected to raise average gross pay for a full-time family physician from $250,000 to $385,000 before overhead. Osborne says attachment is growing faster than population and distinguishes "attachment" from "access"; CCPA's Andrew Longhurst argues higher pay without access gains fits a "target-income" effect and cites Quebec; Conservative health critic Brennan Day says measurement is inadequate. - [The Tyee](https://thetyee.ca/News/2026/05/31/BC-Family-Doctor-Crisis-Gets-Even-Worse/)
- 2026 LFP enrolment total: not found (latest figure is the 2025 one). Ministry asked physicians to re-enrol between 2026-01-01 and 2026-03-31 ([Ministry LFP page](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/msp/physicians/longitudinal-family-physician-lfp-payment-model), via search summary).

ER closures (RECORD / data)
- Conservatives claim ERs closed 2,400 times in two years; no source named on their release, so this is an unverified party claim. - [Conservative BC, Keep Emergency Rooms Open, 2026-10-05](https://conservativebc.ca/keep-emergency-rooms-open/)
- Site-level data: 100 Mile House ER had 17 temporary closures in the first five months of 2026 per Interior Health reporting (via search summary, [CFJC Today](https://cfjctoday.com/2026/01/02/as-emergency-room-closures-continue-in-b-c-paramedics-struggle-to-fill-the-gap/) is the related January item); Doerkson said at 100 Mile House that its ER "has closed more than 30 times" - [Canadian Press via CFJC, 2026-10-05](https://cfjctoday.com/2026/10/05/bc-ndps-tax-policies-draw-conservative-criticism-midway-through-provincial-election-2/). A Castanet piece says closures in places like Clearwater and Merritt have tapered after a spate in 2022-2024 (via search summary, [Castanet](https://www.castanetkamloops.net/news/Kamloops/620686/B.C.s-health-minister-says-rural-ER-closures-are-easing-in-some-Interior-communities)). No independent provincewide tally found.

Wait times (RECORD / data)
- Fraser Institute 2025: BC median total wait from GP referral to treatment was 32.2 weeks, the longest in BC since estimates began in 1993 per its commentary; national median 28.6 weeks; Ontario shortest at 19.2. The measure excludes the wait to see a GP, and the Fraser Institute is a think tank with a stated market-oriented perspective. - [Fraser Institute](https://www.fraserinstitute.org/studies/waiting-your-turn-wait-times-for-health-care-in-canada-2025) (via search summary)
- NDP says surgical waits for some procedures range from 10 to 50-plus weeks depending on the surgeon referred to; 382,000 surgeries completed last year; 21 new MRI units and 15 CT scanners added; MRI exams up 108%, CT up 61%. Party claims. - [BC NDP, Oct 6 release](https://www.bcndp.ca/releases/getting-care-sooner-eby-will-cut-wait-times-add-new-diagnostic-equipment)
- CIHI BC-specific joint replacement figures for 2024/25: not found. Older benchmarks found: BC 2016 hip 61% and knee 47% within 26 weeks (via search summary, [Times Colonist editorial](https://www.timescolonist.com/editorials-archive/editorial-cutting-wait-lists-is-not-impossible-4643961)).

Nurse-patient ratios (RECORD)
- BC was first in Canada to set minimum ratios via a 2023 agreement between the Ministry of Health and the Nurses' Bargaining Association (e.g., 1:4 general medical/surgical, 1:1 ICU). BCNU said in March 2026 that phase one was 73% activated; about $750 million committed through 2026. - [BCNU, 2026](https://www.bcnu.org/news-and-events/news/2026/minimum-nurse-patient-ratios-focus-bc-nurses-conference) (via search summary)

Surrey hospitals (RECORD / data)
- New Surrey (Cloverdale) hospital: under construction, structural work halfway as of 2026-03-06; reported estimate $2.88 billion with completion August 2029 and patients from 2030; 168 beds, 55 emergency treatment spaces, new BC Cancer Centre; earlier estimates were about $1.72 billion with April 2027 completion. Cost/date figures are from a search summary of CTV/other coverage and I could not confirm them on the page itself - [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/vancouver/article/new-surrey-hospital-reaches-construction-milestone/) (page showed headline only); cost-overrun framing at [CTV](https://guelph.ctvnews.ca/vancouver/article/new-surrey-hospital-will-cost-1b-more-than-planned-wont-open-until-2030).
- NDP candidate Jagrup Brar says the hospital is "more than halfway complete," and cites a medical school at SFU Surrey and a BC Cancer Centre; Doerkson, asked about it on 2026-10-07, said "we're contemplating all of that for our platform rollout." - [Indo-Canadian Voice, 2026-10-07](https://voiceonline.com/doerkson-has-no-answer-on-surrey-hospital-more-than-halfway-built-bc-ndp/) (NDP-sourced)
- Surrey Memorial ER: physicians reported 8.4% of patients leaving without being seen, up from 2.9%, and 30% more visits over four years without added space (article undated, likely 2024) - [CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.7325687). One obstetrician said in a 2024 segment the Cloverdale hospital would lack a maternity ward - [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/video/10343497/surreys-health-care-crisis-obstetrician-gynecologist-warns-about-maternity-care-crisis/); unverified for current design.
- SFU School of Medicine in Surrey has started its first cohort (NDP claim). - [BC NDP, Action for You](https://www.bcndp.ca/action-for-you)

Private delivery debate
- 2024 (RECORD of stated position): Rustad-era Conservative plan proposed guaranteed wait times with out-of-province or out-of-country treatment where BC could not meet them, and more publicly funded partnerships with private clinics for procedures and diagnostics; cost not provided. Under the Medicare Protection Act private clinics cannot bill MSP-covered services to patients. - [CBC, 2024](https://amp.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.7268626); [Global News, 2024](https://globalnews.ca/news/10631278/bc-conservatives-hybrid-health-care-system)
- 2026 (PLATFORM): the Conservative "Keep Emergency Rooms Open" release says to "keep BC's health care universal and publicly funded," ensure people need only their health card to get care, and launch an expert commission within 100 days "to bring solutions from other public systems"; no private-clinic or Alberta-model language appears on the release or the BC Ballot list of 12 health items. - [Conservative BC](https://conservativebc.ca/keep-emergency-rooms-open/); [BC Ballot](https://bcballot.ca/platforms/conservative-party/)
- Rustad is not running in 2026 and was removed as leader 2025-12-03 (disputed; he resigned the next day). - [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_British_Columbia_general_election)
- OneBC's plan page reportedly includes "private healthcare alongside public care" (tracker summary) - [BC Ballot index](https://bcballot.ca/platforms/). Primary OneBC source not fetched.
- NDP frames the Conservatives as privatizing ("America Junior"); that is a partisan characterization, not a Conservative commitment - [CFJC/CP, 2026-10-05](https://cfjctoday.com/2026/10/05/bc-ndps-tax-policies-draw-conservative-criticism-midway-through-provincial-election-2/).

PLATFORM by party (2026)
- NDP: 20 new primary care clinics; fast-track international medical graduates already in BC; 1,000 more training seats for nurses, NPs, allied health and care aides; incentives for same-day, evening and weekend appointments - [Global News, 2026-10-09](https://globalnews.ca/news/12097726/bc-leaders-campaign-trail-health-care-supporting-seniors-october-9); 8 MRIs and 10 CTs (about 15% more diagnostic capacity), coordinated regional surgical waitlists, first MRI at Eagle Ridge, robotic surgery investment, BC Health Passport app, US recruitment of surgeons and technologists - [BC NDP, Oct 6](https://www.bcndp.ca/releases/getting-care-sooner-eby-will-cut-wait-times-add-new-diagnostic-equipment); triple the US health-worker recruitment campaign (the earlier summary cited $15 million) - [BC NDP, Sep 23](https://www.bcndp.ca/releases/eby-triple-us-health-worker-recruitment); "no cuts to health care." Funding: new tax bracket above $1 million and +2 points on the top two brackets (changes starting at $190,405), about $1 billion a year, from the 2027 tax year - [CFJC/CP, 2026-10-05](https://cfjctoday.com/2026/10/05/bc-ndps-tax-policies-draw-conservative-criticism-midway-through-provincial-election-2/); [BC Ballot NDP list](https://bcballot.ca/platforms/bc-ndp/). NDP says 96% of British Columbians would pay nothing more (party claim).
- Conservative: keep ERs open, recruit and retain doctors and nurses, $50 million emergency fund for rural practices at risk of closing, train more doctors in BC, bring home Canadians who studied medicine abroad, speed credential recognition, 100-day expert commission on wait times, keep health funding at 2026 budget levels and make health first priority each budget, no user fees, no cuts. - [Conservative BC](https://conservativebc.ca/keep-emergency-rooms-open/); [CFJC/CP](https://cfjctoday.com/2026/10/05/bc-ndps-tax-policies-draw-conservative-criticism-midway-through-provincial-election-2/). No family-doctor attachment target found. Surrey hospital position not stated as of 2026-10-07.
- Green: Integrated Community Health Centre in every riding, finish safe nurse-to-patient ratios, keep public health dollars in public care, cover mental health through public system, single-entry surgical referral, phase out private agency nurses, loan forgiveness and paid practicums for health students, free recommended vaccines, independent health-impact assessment of LNG and fracking, review of the health authority structure - [BC Ballot Green list (Oct 6 release)](https://bcballot.ca/platforms/bc-green-party/); [Green Our Plan](https://bcgreens.ca/our-plan/).
- CentreBC: reduce physician paperwork, expand home care, long-term care homes - [BC Ballot Conservative vs CentreBC](https://bcballot.ca/platforms/conservative-vs-centrebc/).

Fiscal criticism (partisan, with source)
- NDP says the Conservative tax plan "will cost at least $3 billion a year" and force health cuts ([BC NDP releases page](https://www.bcndp.ca/releases)); one report put the NDP estimate of the income-tax change at about $2.5 billion annually (via search summary). Conservatives say the NDP tax bracket changes will "drive doctors out" (CP, above). Doerkson says he is "absolutely committed to keeping those frontline services intact" and that revenue gaps would be covered through cuts and new economic activity - [Daily Hive, 2026-10-08](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/bc-conservatives-election-annual-income-tax-cut-promise).

### Inferences
- INFERENCE: A Conservative government constrained by "no new taxes," a $1,000 tax cut and "no cuts to health" would likely lean on efficiency, recruitment and partnerships rather than structural change; with Rustad gone and the 2026 release silent on private clinics, a Medicare Protection Act amendment looks less likely in a first term than in 2024, but the 100-day commission "bringing solutions from other public systems" leaves room for it. Mark as low confidence.
- INFERENCE: The NDP will likely keep the LFP model and expand team-based care; the new top-bracket revenue (about $1 billion) is small relative to a $36 billion health budget, so the plan implies slower capital timing elsewhere (the 2026 budget already re-paced some long-term care and a Burnaby hospital phase).
- INFERENCE: Greens would prioritize publicly-delivered community health centres, but with 2 seats at dissolution and the accord ended 2026-02-09, their influence depends on a hung result.

## Climate and energy: CleanBC, emissions, carbon tax, LNG, BC Hydro, transmission, wildfire

### Takeaway
BC's legislated 2030 target (40% below 2007) is off track by the province's own accounting; the consumer carbon tax ended 2025-04-01 and industrial pricing continues; the NDP and Conservatives both back LNG expansion (Conservatives: double LNG by 2032 and triple by 2035; NDP: five LNG projects under way or permitted), while the Greens would stop new fossil development.

### Cited Findings

Targets and emissions (RECORD / data)
- Legislated targets: 16% below 2007 by 2025 and 40% below by 2030. The April 2025 accountability report projected emissions only 2.6% below 2007 in 2025 and 20% below in 2030; Energy Minister Adrian Dix: "we are not on track to meet our near-term 2030 goals." - [The Tyee, 2025-05-01](https://thetyee.ca/News/2025/05/01/BC-Admits-Climate-Goals/)
- December 2025 report (2023 data): net emissions down nearly 4% year over year and 9% below 2007; province acknowledged it is not on track for 2030; methane from oil and gas down 48% from 2014, meeting the 2025 methane target early (via search summary) - [news.gov.bc.ca 2025ECS0048](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025ECS0048-001198); report PDF [2025 Climate Change Accountability Report](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/climate-change/action/cleanbc/2025_climate_change_accountability_report.pdf) (not fetched).
- Largest increases (2022 vs 2021) came from oil and gas extraction, domestic aviation and road transport; LNG Canada Phase 2 would add about 2.3 Mt, roughly 5% of provincial emissions, per The Tyee. - [The Tyee](https://thetyee.ca/News/2025/05/01/BC-Admits-Climate-Goals/)
- Independent CleanBC review released 2025-11-26 recommended strengthening industrial carbon pricing, methane targets and the Low Carbon Fuel Standard, adjusting the EV mandate, and integrated energy planning. - [Canadian Climate Institute](https://climateinstitute.ca/news/review-cleanbc-provides-realistic-path-strengthen-economy-cut-emissions/). Government's formal response/decisions: not found.
- NDP claims: emissions per dollar of GDP down over 30%, oil and gas pollution down 10% since 2018, heat pump sales up 63% since 2020, EVs 17.9% of new light-duty sales, 20% of land protected with 30% by 2030 goal. Party claims. - [BC NDP, Action for You](https://www.bcndp.ca/action-for-you)

Carbon tax and replacements (RECORD)
- BC legislated removal of the consumer carbon tax effective 2025-04-01 (it was scheduled to rise $15/tonne annually to $170 by 2030); Eby said large industrial emitters would continue to pay through an effective price. - [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.7493181); [BIV](https://biv.com/news/april-1-carbon-tax-hike-to-be-cancelled-10377827)
- Industrial pricing: the Output-Based Pricing System took effect 2024-04-01 (replacing the CleanBC Industrial Incentive Program), covering facilities over 10,000 tCO2e with a 1% annual tightening rate. - [Blakes](https://www.blakes.com/insights/b-c-implements-new-output-based-pricing-system-for-carbon-emissions/); [IETA](https://www.ieta.org/uploads/wp-content/2025/09/IETA-Business-brief-BC_Sept2025.pdf)
- The tax removal left about a $1.8 billion hole, and the CleanBC Go Electric passenger EV rebate was paused/ended 2025-05-15 and not reinstated per later reporting; BC also lowered its 2035 ZEV target. - [Electric Autonomy](https://electricautonomy.ca/policy-regulations/ev-rebates-incentives-funding/2025-04-30/bc-ev-rebate-program-ending-canada/); [DriveTeslaCanada](https://driveteslacanada.ca/news/british-columbia-lowers-2035-zev-target-expands-charging-network-but-doesnt-bring-back-rebates/) (secondary sources)
- Low Carbon Fuel Standard: amended to require 30% carbon-intensity reduction by 2030; Canadian-content requirement for the 5% renewable fuel in gasoline from 2026-01-01 (via search summary of government sources) - [news.gov.bc.ca 2025ECS0008](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025ECS0008-000147); [IETA LCFS brief](http://www.ieta.org/uploads/wp-content/2025/09/IETA_Business_Brief-BC-Low-Carbon-Fuel-Standard-September_2025.pdf).
- Possible pressure to soften industrial carbon pricing after a Canada-Alberta deal in 2026 is commentary, not a BC decision - [EnergyNow](https://energynow.ca/2026/05/bc-will-be-under-pressure-to-lower-carbon-pricing-to-match-alberta/).

LNG and fossil fuels (RECORD and PLATFORM)
- RECORD: Ksi Lisims LNG received a provincial Environmental Assessment Certificate in September 2025 with 23 enforceable conditions and a net-zero-ready-by-2030 requirement (via search summary) - [news.gov.bc.ca 2025ENV0032](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025ENV0032-000878). LNG Canada Phase 2 (doubling Kitimat to 28 Mtpa) reached a final investment decision in late September 2026 (Shell announcement around 2026-09-29) - [IREI](https://irei.com/news/lng-canadas-jv-partners-take-final-investment-decision-in-phase-2-of-lng-expansion-project/); [CTV/CP](https://www.ctvnews.ca/vancouver/article/ebys-election-campaign-gets-lift-from-lng-canada-announcement/) (NDP taking credit during the campaign). A 2026-09-11 profile still said FID was pending; the later reports supersede it.
- NDP states current BC LNG capacity is 14 Mtpa, with Cedar and Woodfibre under construction and LNG Canada Phase 2, Tilbury Phase 2 and Ksi Lisims permitted and moving toward FID, taking capacity to nearly 48 Mtpa by 2035; says new LNG must be net-zero ready by 2030. Party claim. - [BC NDP, 2026-09-28](https://www.bcndp.ca/releases/bc-conservatives-lng-plan-out-step-reality)
- Conservative PLATFORM: "doubling natural gas production by 2032 and tripling it by 2035," 120 Day Permitting Act for major projects, designate the Montney a strategic provincial asset, work with Ottawa so BC gets emissions credit when LNG displaces coal, bring back LNG companies, First Nations ownership, a "competitive, stable LNG royalty framework"; welcomes a Pacific Link oil pipeline designation and would ask for a trilateral working group with Alberta and Ottawa. - [Conservative BC energy plan](https://conservativebc.ca/plan/make-british-columbia-an-energy-superpower/); [BC Ballot Conservative list](https://bcballot.ca/platforms/conservative-party/). Conflict: one search summary described the pledge as "triple by 2035" only; the party page states both double by 2032 and triple by 2035.
- NDP PLATFORM: "continue supporting LNG expansion," fast-track North Coast Transmission Line (with Canadian steel), double critical mineral exports in five years, cut major mine approval timelines by over 60%. - [BC Ballot NDP list](https://bcballot.ca/platforms/bc-ndp/). Eby has said he is open to a new oil pipeline only if the north coast tanker ban stays (2025 position) - [CTV](https://www.ctvnews.ca/politics/article/bc-premier-open-to-new-pipeline-if-tanker-ban-remains-in-place/); 2026 stance on Pacific Link not confirmed.
- Green PLATFORM: stop new fossil fuel development, end fossil subsidies, require existing LNG projects to pay their own power costs, say no to the oil pipeline, redirect public money from LNG to renewables, independent health-impact assessment of LNG and fracking; no numeric emissions target on the plan page. - [Green Our Plan](https://bcgreens.ca/our-plan/); [BC Ballot Green list](https://bcballot.ca/platforms/bc-green-party/)

Carbon pricing and CleanBC positions of the Conservatives
- 2024 (RECORD of stated position): Rustad's costed platform said it would scrap the carbon price including "hidden carbon taxes" and eliminate the Low Carbon Fuel Standard and CleanBC programs - [Global News, 2024](https://globalnews.ca/news/10812384/affordability-safety-scrapping-carbon-price-bc-conservatives-full-platform); [CHEK, 2024](https://www.cheknews.ca/john-rustad-releases-bc-conservative-partys-climate-plan-1178583/).

The Conservative energy page says "strong environmental protections" for projects but gives no targets.

BC Hydro and electricity supply

- 2025 Call for Power results announced 2026-05-13: 30-year contracts for four wind projects (Bessie 251 MW, Nicola 496 MW, Sweetwater 210 MW, Taylor South 201 MW), about 3,500 GWh/yr, in service by October 2033, with at least 25% First Nations equity; the call drew 14 proposals totalling over 9,100 GWh/yr (via search summary) - [news.gov.bc.ca 2026ECS0014](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026ECS0014-000552). 2024 call: nine wind and one solar project, about 4,830 GWh/yr.
- North Coast Transmission Line: Ottawa and BC committed $3.5 billion (July 2026); line expected to deliver up to 600 MW, including to Ksi Lisims; one source says the estimate for the first two phases doubled from $3 billion to at least $6 billion in 2025. Sources not directly comparable. - [Business Examiner](https://businessexaminer.ca/peace-cariboo-skeena-articles/item/north-coast-transmission-line-funding-debate/)
- Site C: capital cost about $16 billion versus original 2014 budget of about $8.8 billion (BC Hydro lessons-learned report; undated in search results) - [Barchart/CP](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/35875103/bc-hydro-says-it-should-have-been-more-proactive-as-site-c-costs-overflowed).
- NDP claims a $36 billion electricity expansion, 98% clean electricity, and residential rates less than half Alberta's. Party claims. - [BC NDP, Action for You](https://www.bcndp.ca/action-for-you)

## Transit and transportation: Surrey-Langley SkyTrain, Broadway subway, TransLink funding, Massey tunnel, ICBC

### Takeaway
Surrey-Langley SkyTrain is under construction at about $6.0 billion for late-2029 opening; the Massey replacement is now $8.5 billion with 2031 opening; TransLink needs a new revenue source from 2028 and both the NDP and Conservatives have ruled out a vehicle levy; ICBC rates are frozen and the Conservatives propose a "hybrid" injury system with more insurance choice.

- Surrey-Langley SkyTrain: 16 km, eight stations, cost $5.996 billion (up from $4.01 billion in the 2022 business case), opening moved from late 2028 to late 2029; construction active at all eight stations as of May 2026; the June 2026 status report still cites $5.996 billion. - [CTV News, 2024-08-15](https://www.ctvnews.ca/vancouver/article/cost-of-surrey-langley-skytrain-extension-balloons-to-6b/); [SLS June 2026 status report](https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/938/2026/07/SLS_Monthly-Status-Report_June_2026.pdf) (via search summary)

- Broadway subway: not researched (see Gaps).
- TransLink: Mayors' Council previously projected a shortfall of up to $600 million a year from 2026 (later reduced to $365 million after cuts); BC earlier provided $479 million of interim support. TransLink now seeks at least $112 million of new annual revenue from 2028 when provincial operating subsidies end; the province promised legislation in 2027 for a new revenue source from 2028. - [Business Examiner](https://businessexaminer.ca/bc-news/item/eby-rejects-vehicle-levy-as-translink-seeks-112-million-in-new-annual-revenue/) (via search summary); [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/10643685/translink-funding-cuts-worries); [Surrey Now-Leader](https://surreynowleader.com/news/b-c-puts-479m-into-translink-to-keep-fares-low-avoid-service-cuts-premier-eby)
